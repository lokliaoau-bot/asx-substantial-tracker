The WAM Capital (ASX: WAM) investment portfolio decreased in October. Information and communications
technology company Data#3 (ASX: DTL) was a contributor to the investment portfolio performance while
construction materials, equipment and services provider Maas Group Holdings (ASX: MGH) was a detractor.
Data#3’s share price increased in October after the company upgraded its FY2027 half year earnings
expectations twice during the month. The company now expects profit before tax to increase between 55%
and 70% on the prior corresponding period, following continued momentum across its Infrastructure and
Software businesses. Strong demand has been driven by networking, data centre infrastructure and software
solutions, while the company is also benefitting from higher-than-expected interest income and a number of
large transactions during the half year. We remain confident in Data#3’s medium-term outlook as businesses
invest in upgrading their technology infrastructure to support the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI),
increasing demand for hardware, software and services.
Maas Group Holdings’ share price fell sharply during October as Firmus Technologies’ withdrawn initial public
offering (IPO) weighed on investor sentiment. Maas Group Holdings holds a 3.2% stake in Firmus Technologies
and has significant exposure to contracted electrical infrastructure work with the company, including
approximately $700 million in outstanding contracted work, amid uncertainty around Firmus Technologies’
ability to fund it following the withdrawal. Separately, Maas Group Holdings confirmed the completion of the
sale of its Construction Materials division, receiving $1.61 billion in proceeds and repaying its Australian
syndicated bank facilities, which materially strengthened the company's balance sheet. We view the delay in
the IPO market as prudent in the context of broader public equity market conditions which have experienced a
sharp de-rating in AI-exposed sectors. We understand Firmus Technologies is in the process of securing
additional funding in private markets. As one of the only AI factory companies globally with NVIDIA (NASDAQ:
NVDA) offtake support, along with strategic advantages in where capacity is being bought online, this positions
them well and is a key potential catalyst to provide the market with confidence that the broader pipeline will be
executed.
