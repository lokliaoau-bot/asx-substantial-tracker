The WAM Microcap investment portfolio decreased in September. During the month, leading finance broker
aggregator COG Financial Services (ASX: COG) was a contributor to the investment portfolio performance, while
medical technology company Echo IQ (ASX: EIQ) was a detractor.
COG Financial Services is Australia's leading aggregator of finance brokers and equipment leasing
businesses. The company’s share price rose during September, following a stronger -than- expected
FY2026 full year result and expectations for further earnings upgrades. Management is targeting
earnings growth of at least 10% in FY2027, supported by momentum in its salary packaging business
and contributions from recent acquisitions. We believe the company's valuation remains attractive
and could improve as salary packaging becomes a larger part of group earnings. Our positive outlook
is also supported by opportunities for further acquisitions and earnings upgrades. Over the medium
term, improvement in the broader macroeconomic environment could benefit the company’s broking
and aggregation business, which has a significant presence in the small and medium- sized
enterprise (SME) asset finance market.
Echo IQ is an Australian medical technology company focused on improving cardiology decision-
making. The company uses artificial intelligence (AI) to detect structural heart issues from
echocardiograms. Echo IQ’s share price declined during September, following the US Food and Drug
Administration’s (FDA) decision not to grant clearance for EchoSolv HF. EchoSolv HF is the
company’s AI-based clinical decision -support software, designed to assist clinicians in identifying
heart failure. The FDA issued a “Not Substantially Equivalent” determination on the initial application,
delaying US commercialisation. Management noted the FDA's feedback related to statistical analysis
rather than the underlying technology. However, the announcement created uncertainty around the
timing of regulatory clearance and commercialisation. In response, the company outlined multiple
pathways to progress the application and reiterated its confidence in ultimately achieving FDA
clearance.
